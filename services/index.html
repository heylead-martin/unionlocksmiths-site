Understanding Our Services

Emergency Services

Need a solution fast? Wah Luen Locksmiths will despatch a rapid response team to get your business back in operation.

We can get to you promptly - usually within 20-35 minutes - depending on where you are in Singapore.

Upon confirmation, and after the required security checks*, our team will help your staff to gain entry in urgent situations.

We will open your door by picking or drilling the lock, minimising any potentially costly damage to the surrounding door or doorframe, if at all.

If you have an emergency and you need urgent access, we can come to you as quickly as possible.

Emergencies can include:

  • Lost keys
  • Locked out
  • Locked in
  • Damaged or faulty locks
  • Lock repair

Important: We must confirm your identity before opening locks or doors to any premises. We will refuse to gain access to the premises if you do not have official photo ID or proof that you are the rightful, legal owner or approved staff / occupant.
